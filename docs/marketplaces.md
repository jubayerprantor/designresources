# Creative Marketplaces

Not all great design assets are strictly open-source. For high-stakes client deliverables, commercial campaigns, and complex production pipelines, designers often rely on vetted hybrid and subscription powerhouses.

This directory is split into two distinct tiers:
1. **Freemium Hubs:** Platforms with generous, permanent free tiers alongside optional Pro upgrades.
2. **Elite Subscription Marketplaces:** Globally recognized, industry-standard subscription platforms (no obscure or unvetted paywalled sites).
